## Move forward without losing the record
1. Keep original URLs, dates, authorship, licences and source artifacts.
2. Record dependencies, deployment consumers and citations before moving material.
3. Publish a dated transition note and successor link.
4. Test any Station export against declared schemas, versions, coverage and privacy boundaries.
5. Start a new series unless comparability with the old series is established.
6. Decide separately whether a repository should become read-only.

Do not sum planned samples, source responses, judge decisions, retries and repeated snapshots
into one evidence total. They are different units and may overlap.

Future Station results will be indexed from [Explore Evidence](EVIDENCE.md);
historical results will remain attributable to the program that produced them.
