# Where Station is going
[English](ROADMAP.md) · [Français](ROADMAP.fr.md) · [Start here](START_HERE.md)

**Integrate the measurement signal into your infrastructure, or use Station to turn observations into analysis and evidence.**

NeoMundi is developing these two complementary paths on a shared measurement foundation.

## Qualify the measurement
Strengthen repeatability, documented methods and independent review.
Publish evidence with its scope, coverage and limits.

## Make integration easier
Consolidate the API, Python SDK and MCP connector so teams can use the signal in their own tools.
Develop request-response and streaming support with clear compatibility and documentation.

## Build with pilot teams
Test specific professional needs under declared conditions.
Use documented pilot results to shape the product and its integration paths.

## Bring analysis into Station
Develop a dashboard that brings observations, history and evidence into one place.
Help teams examine results in context and follow their own campaigns.

## Grow into each profession
Build profession-specific protocols and views on the shared foundation.
The direction is **a station for each profession**, informed by qualified pilots.
